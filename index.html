Welcome to ECG Examiner

ECG Examiner is a free, browser-based tool designed to help you view, explore, and better understand electrocardiogram (ECG) recordings — no installation required.

Whether you're a medical student learning the basics, a healthcare professional reviewing recordings, or simply someone curious about how the heart works, this tool gives you a clear and interactive way to work with ECG data.

What can you do here?
  • 📂 Upload an ECG — supports XML files (GE MUSE, Biocare and similar formats) for full digital analysis, as well as ECG images (JPG, PNG) for visual exploration
  • 🔍 Zoom and explore — navigate the recording in detail, measure signal values in real time
  • 📊 Automated analysis — instantly see Heart Rate, PR Interval, QRS Duration and QTc Interval
  • 🫀 Advanced views — explore individual beats, vectorcardiograms, lead comparisons and interval charts
  • 📄 Export reports — generate a PDF summary with patient information and findings

⚠️ Important: This tool is intended for educational and informational purposes only. It is not a certified medical device and should not be used as a substitute for professional clinical judgment.
